00WELCOME

Thank you so much for your interest in CUSTOM YOUR NARUSASU!

We are inviting NaruSasu artists from around the world to contribute to a special postcard and acrylic charm collection for a fan event in Tokyo on July 18, 2027. Please read the details before deciding whether to participate.

01DISTRIBUTION

Finished items will be distributed for a fee, not only given away.

IMPORTANT — Paid distribution and no monetary compensation

The postcards and acrylic charms will be used (1) as prizes in a paid randomized event activity (¥500 per play: 2 postcards + 1 charm), and (2) in paid COMPLETE BOX sets containing all postcard and charm designs for those who prefer not to receive random items. Sales at the venue and online are planned. No monetary fee or royalties will be paid to contributors. Finished items will be provided as gifts, and international shipping of contributor gifts will be covered by the organizer.

This is an independently organized, unofficial NARUTO × SASUKE fan project and is not affiliated with the rights holders.

02CONCEPT

Your own NaruSasu, in your own style.

There is no fixed scene or story prompt. Please create your own interpretation of Naruto × Sasuke. We would love to bring together many different expressions of NaruSasu from around the world.

All-ages artwork only

Naruto × Sasuke only. R18/explicit sexual content and excessive gore beyond the tone of the original work are not permitted.

03PARTICIPATION

Select what you would like to create. The corresponding guidelines will appear below.

Charm designs are limited (approximately 4–6 designs planned). Charm and BOTH applications may close earlier than the artwork deadline.
